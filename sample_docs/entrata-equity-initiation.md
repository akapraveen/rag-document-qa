# Entrata (ENT): equity initiation

**Constructive. Base target price $16.50 per share** (sensitivity range about $15–$18).

An initiation-style view of Entrata ahead of its IPO, built from the S-1/A: a units × ARPU revenue
build, a linked three-statement model, and a relative valuation against vertical-SaaS peers.

## Thesis
- **Large, under-digitised market.** About $1T of annual rent across 23.4M U.S. multifamily units; Entrata
  powers about 2.5M (roughly 10%).
- **Enterprise validation.** 233 customers above $500K ARR make up 84% of ARR.
- **Unified data and AI flywheel.** Property management, payments, resident experience and the ELI+ AI layer
  on one data model raise switching costs and ARPU without needing new units.

## Model
| $M | 2024A | 2025A | 2026E | 2027E | 2028E |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 412 | 509 | 612 | 720 | 832 |
| Growth | – | 24% | 20% | 18% | 16% |
| Gross margin | 56% | 60% | 62% | 63% | 64% |
| Non-GAAP operating margin | 14% | 23% | 26% | 28% | 30% |
| Adjusted FCF margin | 2% | 16% | 17% | 19% | 20% |

Revenue = units × ARPU. Units grow 11% → 10% → 9% as the base scales; ARPU grows 8% → 7% → 6% on
cross-sell, payments and ELI+, reaching $256 in 2028E against a ~$580 benchmark for high-ARPU customers.

## Valuation
| Method | Multiple | Price / share | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026E EV / Revenue | 5.7× | $16.15 | 50% |
| 2027E EV / Revenue, discounted one year | 5.5× | $16.71 | 30% |
| 2026E EV / Non-GAAP operating income | 22× | $16.21 | 20% |
| **Weighted** (less $270M net debt, 199.2M diluted shares) | | **$16.33 → $16.50** | |

The 5.7× multiple is a premium to the 4.7× median of the selected vertical-SaaS set (AppFolio, CoStar,
Procore, Guidewire, Tyler, ServiceTitan), supported by 117% net retention, a Rule of 40 near 47% and the
enterprise mix. Horizontal and HCM SaaS names (Monday.com, BILL, Paycom, Paylocity) are kept as a cross-check only.

## Risks to monitor
- Gross retention slipping from 99% to 97%.
- Lumpy embedded-payments and transaction revenue.
- Final IPO share count, dilution and the controlled-company structure.
